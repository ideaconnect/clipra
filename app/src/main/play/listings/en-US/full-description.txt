Clipra is a free video editor for your phone and tablet, with every feature unlocked and no watermark on your videos. It also keeps your videos to yourself: no account, no cloud, and every AI feature runs on your device.

NO WATERMARK, EVER
Every video you export is clean, with no watermark and no logo. That holds for free users too, at every resolution.

EVERY FEATURE FREE
No trial, no subscription and no feature behind a paywall: layers, edit by text, auto captions, every AI tool and 4K export are all free. Clipra shows two fullscreen ads per export, one when it starts and one when it is done, and never while you edit. Clipra Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the ads, and that is all it changes.

A REAL TIMELINE WITH LAYERS
Put anything on any layer: video, photos, text, captions, sound. Higher layers cover lower ones, so anything can go over anything: picture in picture is a smaller clip on a higher layer, a title is text over your video, and a person cut out of their background stands over any scene on the layer below. Every layer plays sound and can be hidden, muted or locked. Move, scale and turn a clip right in the preview, with 16 blend modes, opacity, crop and keyframes for position, scale, rotation and masks. Split, trim, snap and ripple on the timeline, with undo and redo for every step.

TEXT AND TITLES
Titles, text cards and lower thirds over your video, with text effects, colours, cards, animations in and out, and templates you save yourself.

EDIT BY TEXT
Clipra writes down what is said and lets you edit the video like a document. Cut filler words in one tap, remove long pauses, or tap any word to cut it. Everything after stays in sync on every layer.

AUTO CAPTIONS
Captions made on your phone with Whisper, in the language spoken. Five styles, including karaoke that highlights each word. Fix words, split and merge captions, translate them offline, and export or import .srt files.

AI ON YOUR DEVICE
- Voice cleanup removes wind, traffic and room noise
- Cutout removes the background behind people, so you can put them over any video, or use a chroma key
- Follow face keeps a mask on a face, for blurring or highlighting
- Auto-reframe makes a vertical, square or 4:5 copy that follows the subject
- Stabilize smooths handheld shake
AI models are downloaded once, straight from their official sources, and then work offline.

EVERYTHING ELSE YOU NEED
- 24 filters and 24 transitions
- Color adjustments, HSL and LUT files
- Speed from 0.25x to 4x, speed ramps, freeze frame and reverse
- Loudness normalization, fades, ducking under speech, beat markers and voiceover
- 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 4:3 and 21:9 projects, and mosaic split-screen layouts
- Tablets get a full three-pane layout

EXPORT
Up to 4K at up to 60 fps, in H.264, HEVC or AV1 where your phone has the encoder. Clipra shows the output size and the estimated file size before you export. You can also save the cover, the sound, the captions and an OpenTimelineIO file of the project.

PRIVACY FIRST
Your videos, transcripts and edits never leave your phone. Clipra uses the network only to download AI models (on Wi-Fi by default), to show ads to free users and for Google Play purchases.
